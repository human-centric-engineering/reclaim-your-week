# Time Audit Tool: User Prompts

**How to use this document:** Once your Claude Project is set up, open a new conversation inside it and copy and paste one of the prompts below, filling in any items in square brackets.

## Prompt 1 — First Time User

Use this the very first time you run your time audit. Claude will guide you through a reflective exploration of how you spend your time, with the option to upload your calendar data for a deeper, evidence-based analysis partway through.

> I'm ready to begin my time audit. This is my first time using this tool. Please guide me through the full process from the beginning.

**What happens next:** Claude will welcome you, explain the process, and guide you through each phase step by step. Allow 30-60 minutes of uninterrupted time.

**Tip:** During the audit, Claude will offer you the option to upload your calendar data for a reality check. If you would like to do this, it helps to have your calendar export ready in advance. In Google Calendar, go to Settings, then Import & export, then Export. You will download a .zip file containing a .ics file. Have this saved on your computer before you start, and you can upload it when prompted. This step is optional but recommended.

## Prompt 2 — Resuming After An Interruption

Use this if you were partway through your audit and the original conversation thread is no longer accessible. Your first option should always be to return to the same conversation — just scroll back and continue. Claude will have everything it needs. If that is not possible, use this prompt in a new conversation inside the Time Audit project, and paste in the checkpoint summary Claude produced at the end of your last completed phase.

> I was partway through my time audit and need to resume. Here is my checkpoint summary from where I left off: [Paste your checkpoint summary here]. Please continue from where we left off.

**Note:** If you do not have a checkpoint summary, briefly describe which phase you completed and the key answers you gave. The more context you paste in, the better Claude can pick up the thread.

## Prompt 3 — Returning User

Use this when you have completed a full audit before and are returning to check your progress, whether a month, a quarter, or a year later. If you uploaded your previous audit summary to the Files section of your project, Claude will already have it — just use the short version of this prompt. If not, use the longer version and paste your previous summary in. Claude will guide you through a fresh audit, including the option to upload current calendar data, and compare your results to last time.

**If your previous summary is uploaded to the project:**

> I have done this time audit before and I'm ready to run it again to see how things have changed. You should have my previous results in the project files. Please review them, then guide me through a fresh audit and compare my results to last time so I can celebrate progress and keep going.

**If your previous summary is not uploaded:**

> I have done this time audit before and I'm ready to run it again to see how things have changed. Here is a summary of my previous audit results:
>
> [Paste your previous audit summary here]
>
> Please guide me through a fresh audit, and at the end compare my results to last time so I can see what has shifted and what I need to work on now.

**Tip:** The summary artifact Claude produced at the end of your previous audit is the ideal thing to paste in here. If you shared your results with Rashmir, you may also have a copy in your email.
