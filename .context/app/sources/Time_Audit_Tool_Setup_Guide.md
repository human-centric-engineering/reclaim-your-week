# Your Time Audit: Getting Started

Welcome. You are about to do something many people never do: look honestly at how you are spending your time so that you can make intentional choices going forward.

This guide walks you through setting up your personal Time Audit tool. It takes about 10 minutes to set up, and around 30-45 minutes to run the audit itself. Set aside uninterrupted time for both.

## What You Will Need

- Approximately 60 minutes of quiet, focused time
- A Claude Pro account (see Step 1 if you don't have one yet, else go to Step 2)
- The System Prompt file, which is linked in Step 3, i.e. when you need it
- Your calendar export file (optional but recommended). If you use Google Calendar, go to Settings, then Import & export, then Export. Save the downloaded .zip file and unzip it to find your .ics file. You can upload this during the audit for a deeper analysis. Claude will also guide you through this step if you prefer to do it during the session rather than downloading it in advance.

## Step 1 — Create or Upgrade Your Claude Account

The Time Audit tool runs inside Claude Projects, which requires a Claude Pro account ($20/month).

**If you don't have a Claude account:**

1. Go to claude.ai
2. Click Sign up and create a free account
3. Once logged in, click Upgrade to Pro and follow the steps

**If you already have a free Claude account:**

1. Log in at claude.ai
2. Click Upgrade to Pro in the left sidebar
3. Follow the steps to upgrade

**If you already have Claude Pro:**

You are ready. Move to Step 2.

## Step 2 — Create a New Project

Projects are Claude's way of holding a persistent context so that every time you open this tool, it already knows its role and how to guide you.

1. Log in to claude.ai
2. In the left sidebar, look for Projects and click it
3. Click New Project
4. Name it: My Time Audit
5. You do not need to enter a description in the box below, but if you want to, you can paste the following: A structured time audit tool to help purpose-driven leaders reclaim space for their most important work.
6. Click Create project

You will now be inside your new project.

## Step 3 — Add the System Prompt

This is the most important step. You are going to upload the document that contains the tool's instructions. Claude will read it automatically every time you start a conversation inside this project.

1. Open the file called Time Audit Tool: System Prompt Text Version, which is available at this link
2. Select all the text (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on Mac) and copy it
3. Inside your project, click the + next to Instructions
4. Paste the text in and save

## Step 4 — Run Your Audit

1. Open the document called Time Audit Tool: User Prompts, which you can access at this link
2. Choose the prompt that matches your situation and copy and paste it into a new chat inside your Time Audit project
3. Press enter and follow Claude's lead.

**Note:** Claude will guide you through the full process. You will start with an exploration of how you spend your time, then be offered the option to upload your calendar data for a reality check against your estimates. Whether or not you upload calendar data, you will receive a visual summary and a prioritised action plan. Feel free to be discursive as you work with Claude. The goal is to help you generate insights and useful next steps. Providing information to Claude helps Claude help you.

## A Few Things to Keep in Mind Before You Start

### Be honest. Ask questions and engage.

The audit is only as useful as the honesty you bring to it. Claude will not judge you. What you see in the data is there to help you, not to assess you. Don't be afraid to ask questions, engage and even challenge Claude. This will help you get insight and results that are truly useful to you.

### Set aside the time.

The audit takes around 30-60 minutes. Do it in one sitting if you can as the conversation builds on itself and is richer when you do not break it up.

### Have your calendar nearby.

Having your calendar visible can help you answer questions about how your time is distributed. You will also be offered the option to upload your calendar data for a deeper analysis. If you would like to do this, have your export file ready in advance (see What You Will Need above). Alternatively, Claude will guide you through the export process during the session if you prefer.

### Wondering which Claude model to use?

The default is fine. However, if you plan to upload your calendar data, switch to Claude Opus at High effort. Click the model name next to the send button, select Opus, and make sure the effort level shows High. Leave it there for the rest of the audit. (This does burn through your message allowance faster, so change this back once you've completed the audit.)

### You will get a summary at the end.

Claude will produce a clean summary document that you can copy, save, or share. If you would like to share your results with Rashmir, either ahead of a coaching session or to request a consultation, send them to rashmir@rashmir.net.

**Please note:** if you choose to share your results, they may be used in line with Rashmir's privacy and data policy, i.e. they may inform analysis and patterns identification across Rashmir's client base and inform future research and writing. Your individual data will always remain confidential.

### Save and upload your results.

At the end of the audit, Claude will produce a summary. Copy and save this somewhere safe. If you plan to run the audit again in the future, upload your saved summary to the Files section of your Time Audit project (click the + next to Files). This means Claude will be able to see your previous results automatically next time, without you needing to paste anything in.

## If Something Does Not Work

**Claude does not seem to know what a time audit is:**
You are likely in a regular chat, not inside your Project. Go back to Projects in the left sidebar, open your Time Audit project, and start a new conversation there.

**Claude's response doesn't make sense or a chart doesn't render correctly:**
Tell Claude, and ask it to fix it or redo the response.

**You get stuck:**
Ask Claude for help. If useful, share a picture of what you are stuck on with Claude and ask for guidance. And of course, feel free to reach out to Rashmir when needed.

**You got interrupted mid-audit:**
First, try to return to the same conversation thread — just scroll back up and keep going. Claude will have the full context of everything you said and can pick up exactly where you left off. If that conversation is no longer accessible, start a new conversation inside the same project and use the Resuming prompt from the User Prompts document, pasting in the checkpoint summary Claude produced at the end of your last completed phase. The more context you give, the better Claude can reconstruct the thread.

**You want to redo the audit in the future:**
Start a new conversation inside the project using the Returning User prompt. If you uploaded your previous summary to the Project's Files section, Claude will already have your previous results and can compare automatically. If not, paste your previous summary into the prompt. Each conversation is independent, so your previous audit is still saved in its own conversation history.
